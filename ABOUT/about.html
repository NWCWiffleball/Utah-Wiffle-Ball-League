About The NWC

At the NWC, our mission is to grow and celebrate the wonderful sport of Wiffle ball through free tournaments, community engagement, and family-centered competition. We believe Wiffle ball is more than just a game — it is a way to bring people together, create lasting memories, and strengthen communities through healthy play and positive experiences.

We are passionate supporters of the family unit and believe sports should be accessible and enjoyable for all ages. From young children picking up a bat for the first time to experienced adult competitors, we strive to create events that welcome everyone regardless of age or skill level.

The NWC hosts competitive tournaments and community events designed to encourage sportsmanship, teamwork, and active lifestyles. Whether participants are playing for fun, family bonding, or serious competition, our goal is to provide an exciting and inclusive environment where everyone can enjoy the game.

Through partnerships with local communities, organizations, and supporters, we aim to enrich the communities we serve while continuing to promote and expand the sport of Wiffle ball. Every tournament is an opportunity to unite players, families, and fans around a shared love for the game.

At its core, the NWC is committed to growing the sport, supporting families, encouraging healthy recreation, and building stronger communities — one tournament at a time.
