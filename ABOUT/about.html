About The NWC

August of 2024 I moved up to Utah to attend college at Weber State. After competing and playing in wiffle ball tournaments the previous year, I wanted to continue to do so and set up a league in Utah, so I made a post in a variety of Facebook Groups to no avail.

I did end up having one person reach out to me, that being Cameron Grant, the team captian of Team 1 and Co-Commissioner with myself. Silently in the background, me and him were trying our best to find somethng that would work, and find people that would be interested. But nothing we tried seem to work.

That is, until I found out about a wiffle ball slow pitch tournamnet group called Cliffleball! And I attended plenty of them with extended family, and they inspired me to really push for the league to be a reality. So, I just decided to ask some of my closest friends and convinced them to come out and try it.

Luckily enough, almost all of them had a fun enough time to do it again a few weeks later, and that's when I realized we could get this thing started. It'l be small, but it would FINALLY become a reality. So I asked those friends and they said they'd be down! Along with Cameron asking a few of his buddies, we have started up with 12 people split into 3 teams of 4!

Having just completed our draft on June 6th, 2026, after almost 2 years of wanting this to come to fruition, it finally did. We hope you enjoy and support this brand new league!

Sincerly,
Krew Richter
Commissioner of the Utah Wiffle Ball League
